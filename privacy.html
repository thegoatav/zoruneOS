privacy policy

This starter policy explains what the zoruneOS preview stores and why. The preview is operated by its project owner; add a real contact address before inviting the public.

Information stored

  • Account details: username, display name, email address, and a salted password hash. Passwords are not stored in readable form. Email addresses are private and are not shown in chat.
  • Profile details you choose to add: pronouns, bio, status, profile photo, banner, and equipped cosmetic.
  • Service activity: chat messages, role, coin balance, reward claims, and basic account timestamps.
  • On this device: settings, notes, tasks, calendar items, app drafts, and some Browser data may be saved in browser storage. Uploaded music, files, and photos are handled locally by the app unless you send or publish them yourself.

How information is used and shown

Account details are used to sign you in and maintain your account. Your public profile fields, status, name effect, and chat messages can be seen by other zoruneOS members. We do not intentionally publish your email or password hash. Moderators and the service owner can manage chat messages and member roles.

Browser and outside services

The Browser sends web traffic through the zoruneOS server’s Wisp proxy to the websites you visit. The destination websites receive connection requests and may collect information under their own policies. The proxy may process connection details to deliver pages; this preview does not provide a browsing-history dashboard. Movies may contact TMDB for catalog information. Games loads the LuminSDK library. These outside services have their own privacy practices.

Storage, security, and retention

In this preview, account, profile, reward, and chat records are stored by the server in its data file. Passwords use Node.js scrypt hashing. Browser and device app data may remain in local browser storage until cleared. No email verification, password-reset email, or automated retention schedule is configured yet. A public deployment should use durable, access-controlled storage, backups, and a documented retention period.

Your choices

You may edit or remove profile details in the Profile app. The Profile app can delete your account; doing so also deletes your profile and chat messages from the current server data. Device-only app data can be cleared in your browser. To ask a question or request help, contact the service owner: [service owner contact needed].
